Questions & Answers

The honest version.

Is this a real sponsorship? ▾
Yes. The idea is simple: a real iPhone 17 Pro Max, real vinyl stickers and a real founder carrying the phone. This page is the front-end prototype; payments and sponsor onboarding can be connected when you launch.
Why the iPhone 17 Pro Max? ▾
Because this is the phone I'll actually use every day. It becomes the physical surface carrying the sponsor logos, while its camera also helps document the journey.
What does a sponsor get? ▾
A die-cut vinyl sticker of your logo on the chosen placement, a sponsor listing on this page with your link, and the story of your brand physically travelling with my phone. I won't promise impressions or ROI; the promise is the physical placement and the journey.
How does sponsorship payment work? ▾
The current page is a working front-end prototype with demo bid data. For the real launch, payment can be connected to UPI/card checkout and the winning sponsor can submit their logo after payment.
What if another sponsor bids more? ▾
You can bid again while the sponsorship window is open. The highest confirmed bid for a placement gets that spot.
Can any brand join? ▾
Almost. Every sponsor is approved by hand before anything appears, and I keep the final say on what goes on the phone — it travels with me, after all. If your bid is refused, your deposit comes back in full.
Why make the phone part of a campaign? ▾
I'm a solo founder. Instead of quietly buying the phone, I'm turning the purchase into a public build-in-public campaign where supporters can help fund it and become part of the phone's story.
Can I do this with my own phone? ▾
This page is specifically for my iPhone 17 Pro Max sponsorship. A marketplace version for other founders would be a separate idea.
